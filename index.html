Questions

Which plan should I start with?

If you train three days a week or you’re still building your base, start with Moderate. If you already train consistently and can commit to five days, go Intense.

What equipment do I need?

A standard commercial gym. Every exercise has a listed swap in case your gym doesn’t have the machine.

How do I get the PDF?

Checkout happens on Whop. Right after payment you can download the guide, and you can download it again anytime from your Whop account.

How do I open it on my phone?

Right after payment you get two files. Tap PHONE to read the full plan on your phone in one scroll. Tap PRINT if you want to print the logs.

Are there exercise videos?

Yes. Every exercise in the PDF links to a short video demo. Tap the exercise name on your phone, or scan the code next to it if you print the plan.

Is it for men and women?

Yes. The plan is built on effort targets, so every lifter chooses loads that fit their own strength.

No. The plans are digital files you get right after payment, so all sales are final. If your file doesn't arrive or won't open, email help@teamnofat.com and we'll fix it.

I need help with my order.

Email us at help@teamnofat.com and we will get back to you.

I have an injury. Is this for me?

The programs are for healthy adults. If you have an injury or a medical condition, speak with a qualified clinician before starting.
