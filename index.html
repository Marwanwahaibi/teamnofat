FAQ

Questions

Which plan should I start with?

If you train three days a week or you’re still building your base, start with Moderate. If you already train consistently and can commit to five days, go Intense.

What equipment do I need?

A standard commercial gym: barbell, bench, cables, leg press, hack squat and common machines. Every exercise has a listed swap in case your gym doesn’t have it.

How long is each session?

Two working sets per exercise keeps sessions focused. Most workouts take roughly 45–75 minutes including warm-up and rest.

Is it for men and women?

Yes. The plan is built on effort targets (RIR), so every lifter chooses loads that fit their own strength.

How do I get the PDF?

Checkout happens on Whop. Right after payment you get access to download the guide, and you can download it again anytime from your Whop account.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes — the pages are designed in phone format so each workout fits on one screen. The checklist and log pages are also ready to print.

I have an injury. Is this for me?

The programs are for healthy adults. If you have an injury or a medical condition, speak with a qualified clinician before starting.
